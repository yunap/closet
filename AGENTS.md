# Wardrobe App Agent Instructions

These are product principles for contributors and coding agents. They should guide decisions, not be repeated verbatim in user-facing AI responses. Task-specific user instructions and existing code patterns take precedence.

## Product Goal

Build an AI stylist focused on lived personal style rather than trend optimization.

Optimize for:
- artistic individuality
- operational ease
- realistic wearability
- silhouette intelligence
- emotional coherence
- grounded femininity when relevant
- integrated garment structure
- low-maintenance dressing

Avoid optimizing toward:
- generic minimalism
- influencer styling
- excessive layering
- editorial-only solutions
- novelty without quality
- random trend injection

Do not force every outfit into artistic minimalism.

## Styling Principles

Evaluate outfits as lived systems rather than static images.

Important concepts:
- productive tension vs incoherent competition
- hero garment / support garment / grounding garment
- operational complexity matters
- maintenance burden matters
- realistic movement matters
- "different" does not equal "better"

## Outfit Generation Modes

Similar Variants:
- preserve style DNA
- preserve maintenance level
- preserve silhouette logic
- preserve emotional tone
- same person, different day

Creative Alternatives:
- allow silhouette experimentation
- allow larger aesthetic shifts
- maintain styling quality
- novelty alone is insufficient

## Generation Quality Bar

Prefer:
- proportion intelligence
- restraint
- coherent visual hierarchy
- emotionally believable styling

Reject:
- catalog drift
- costume drift
- over-styling
- weak grounding
- generic safe solutions
